here I am writng this moment down to look back at it and reflect, at some distant point in the future.
Its been three months since i last updated my life here, in this 'repository to be' of sorts. My presence here is entirely subject to the creative urges that dictate my being every now and then. I cant force it, discipline it or even cause the slighest bit of change in its consistency with respect to its occurence. And i guess i am happy that way. For its beyond my control and that acceptance sets me free in a very strange sense. I don't determine when this urge resufaces from the depths of my thoughts. All i have managed to learn voluntarily so far is how i ride this creative wave and give myself direction in the endless ocean that human emotions can be.
SInce the past 2-3 months i stopped writing my life down, here as well as in my daily diary. Although its been almost 7 years since i started experiencing the urge, the compulsion to write down thoughts / journal myself; this time the compulsions inside dictated otherwise.
THE CAUSE - Of late (the last year or so) life made me heal places inside my heart, i did't know were existing in the first place. I discovered how in the guise of managing my emotions, regulating my behaviour, dictating my expression of thought and speech, I had become used to control. And oh my, how it silently feeds fuel to your subconscious ego. Its terifying how it all happens and how much you're kept in the dark to your self. You risk becoming a meglomaniac and control freak only more sophisticated and hard to track. The other shocking bit is that in my case I was the prime victim for, my heart has some pretty rigid and deep rooted values that often save from becoming a silent dictator in other people's lives. Thank the heavens for I just can't accept being a gaslighter who manipulates people in the least. Had i not had the principles i hold dearer than life, this skill set would hsure have come in handy. 
When you're able to master your emotions and be the absolute determinant of your expression and behaviour especially in face of powerful enemies like rage, anger, fear and frustration, you develop a hidden kind of strength. One that makes you master of your instincts. You learn to respond and not react to life. To compell your instincts to momentarily subdue and listen to whatever rationalism the mind can conjure in that moment is a dangerous skill. Its like getting the ability to project a calm surface when deep beneath you're a raging storm.
Once you become aware about how you can employ discernment in the matters of the heart and day to day emotional surges, you feel pride. Oh boy, you bet that pride slowly seeps into your consciousness and makes you intoxicated perhaps like whisky in your bloodstream on an empty stomach.
