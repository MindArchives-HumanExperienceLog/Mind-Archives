here I am writng this moment down to look back at it and reflect, at some distant point in the future.
Its been three months since i last updated my life here, in this 'repository to be' of sorts. My presence here is entirely subject to the creative urges that dictate my being every now and then. I cant force it, discipline it or even cause the slighest bit of change in its consistency with respect to its occurence. And i guess i am happy that way. For its beyond my control and that acceptance sets me free in a very strange sense. I don't determine when this urge resufaces from the depths of my thoughts. All i have managed to learn voluntarily so far is how i ride this creative wave and give myself direction in the endless ocean that human emotions can be.
SInce the past 2-3 months i stopped writing my life down, here as well as in my daily diary. Although its been almost 7 years since i started experiencing the urge, the compulsion to write down thoughts / journal myself; this time the compulsions inside dictated otherwise.
THE CAUSE - Of late (the last year or so) life made me heal places inside my heart, i did't know were existing in the first place. I discovered how in the guise of managing my emotions, regulating my behaviour, dictating my expression of thought and speech, I had become used to control. And oh my, how it silently feeds fuel to your subconscious ego. Its terifying how it all happens and how much you're kept in the dark to your own self. You risk becoming a meglomaniac and control freak but only more sophisticated and hard to track.  
When you're able to master your emotions and be the absolute determinant of your expression and behaviour especially in face of powerful enemies like rage, anger, fear and frustration, you develop a hidden kind of strength. One that makes you master of your instincts. You learn to respond and not react to life. To compell your instincts to momentarily subdue and listen to whatever rationalism the mind can conjure in that moment is a dangerous skill. Its like getting the ability to project a calm surface when deep beneath you're a raging storm.
Once you become aware about how you can employ discernment in the matters of the heart and day to day emotional surges, you feel pride. Oh boy, you bet that pride slowly seeps into your consciousness and makes you intoxicated perhaps like whisky in your bloodstream on an empty stomach. And it is this intoxication that came to light. I observed how every time i planned thing and made myself subject to strict discipline and routines, the control freak inside me was growing. I didn't want to live trying to control life. I want myself to have the freedom to go with the flow - and not be judged for it. To not be subject to the controlling tendencies of my very mind. So I rose up in an internal revolution where my heart and soul stood up against the tyrant my mind had become. As a small step I stoopped writing my day to day life. No tracking my routines, no noting down thoughts. Just me being in the moment and flowing witht the chaotic waves within. 
It was in the moment a further realisation dawned on me. With all this control tied around my very neck - my being was deprived of an essential ingridient of life - Chaos. I was focing feeding myself too much order. and so the balance between order and chaos of natural life was completely destroyed, That unnatural absence of chaos had done enough harm. Therefore - the compulsions inside dictated to distance myself from all that provided my being with order. That didn't mean stop doing stuff like going to gym or following a good sleep cycle, it just meant learning to ride that choas, embracing it and nuturing it. 
Without chaos i was stuck with my old self for too long, but thanks to life, with these realisations and the consequent set of actions that sent me into a choatic spiral,  a new self was born - not out of order but out of the very choas i was shunning out. 
This is me - my evolved self. one that has an improved balance betweeen order and choas. one that is much more stronger and dangerous than the previous guy.
