There is a fundamental shift in the way I am choosing to live right now. I am in the mood to give up on everything and rant like a spoilt little kid. I could go into heavy detail and I will. 
With the particular kind and quality of the skills I possess, I housed a sense of superiority - about intellect, analysis and understanding of things. Combine these with a strong will and heart - the byproduct is a self validated and free thinking man. Although it seems right to say that I am independent of the "need" of external validation, yet I still consciously want it at times. To establish competence in the various manifestations of the socio-status hierarchy. To silently know it in your bones that you're better and feel the weight of your potential at all times is hell of a burden. As of now, I want to shed all of that.
For a long time I have compromised my very basic freedoms for instance to speak what I feel without strategising the conversation to accomodate multiple fragile and competing egos. While this long standing enterprise of compromise and accomodation of egos may have proclaimed the noble aim of maintaining peace and essentially management of extremity prone actors in a close space; it is perhaps substantiaited by the realism of "When in Rome, you do as the Romans did" or "My house my rules" (only in this case you're the tourist and the tenant). 
And yet time has led me to feel like taking out a full scale frontal assault on each of those very fragile mostly male egos; festering in the supposedly sanctimonious space - families. I myself am not very attached to material wealth and comforts of luxury. Therefore the silent implications of being under some sort of never ending unpayable gratitude to the familial authority responsible for it is a bit much. I understand that material privlege is genuinely something to be grateful for, however it should not be allowed to impede other values in your own hierarchy of valuses just because it sits at the top in someone else's ladder. The sheer inability to recongize this differnce from those sitting at the top of familial hierarchy chains is what fundamentally causes friction which eventually leads to conflicts ( particularly the unhealthy and potentially permanent kind). There's more important things than material safety and privelege - Freedom, Romanticism, Fearless living, Being in a space that isn't surrounded by heaviness of tense air around it, free of political and strategic moves within the family setup. 
It is precisely moments like this when, the human inside may take over all other sides of your social existence- be it a son/ daughter, a brother, a family member. These tags start feeling empty and the lack of true human connection breaks the illusion of implied promixity via relationship narratives that ever so willingly socio-culturally validated and preserved. The absence of actual bonding between two independent humans and instead between manufactured roles is what makes us less human and more of a false character in a play. The authority of this play is beyond questioning and its legitimacy is sold at a scale that humanity was equipped to handle. It has now become sentient by its own accord and has a life of its own. Its the only correct way of spending a non renewable ever scarce resource - time.
