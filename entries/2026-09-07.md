There is a weirdly reassuring phenomenon that happens when you find yourself showing up again and again throughout a long stretch of time. It doesn't matter how inconsistent you have been during that time period, rather the fact that even after long you find yourself at it again. While the mind might be tempted to critique this moment as justifying inconsistency or accepting mediocrity, one must realize a deeper truth. And that truth is that, this moment is not one for critical introspection but rather a symbol of not giving up. It shows that in the entire vastness of the human experience there is something that you keep showing up for. Something that has stuck with you enough to invariably make you come knocking at its door again. Why this is worthy of your own respect, if not someone else's, is because in today's age and time its so bloody easy to drown in stimulants and be swept away in the strong current of distractions and convenient life paths.
